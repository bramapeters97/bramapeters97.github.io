# Privacy information

Draft prepared 4 November 2026 for owner review before release.

Amstroll is a local-first Amsterdam outing app. No Amstroll account, password, advertising identifier or analytics SDK is required.

## On your device

A local app preference remembers whether the welcome splash has been shown. Your outing preferences, saved ideas, active outing and journal notes are stored in the app's private storage. A photo you select with Apple's photo picker is copied into the app's private storage for that journal memory. Amstroll does not request access to your entire photo library and does not upload your journal notes or photographs. Device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Use the app's reset control to remove its local preferences, saved ideas, active outing, journal entries and copied photographs. Uninstalling the app removes its local container; separately retained device backups follow Apple's backup controls.

## Network requests

Nearby places contacts public place-data services. Depending on the available source, this can include English and Dutch Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap/Overpass, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and Apple Maps. Requests contain a selected starting coordinate or search area; if you explicitly choose your current location, that coordinate can identify where you are. Services also receive normal request information such as an IP address. Linked place images load from their credited image hosts.

Optional weather requests send a selected coordinate to MET Norway. Optional municipal stop requests use Amsterdam's public tram/metro inventory. Nearby recommendations automatically send the selected start, candidate destinations and any chosen finish to Apple’s map services to check walking routes. Tapping an external GVB, NS or directions link opens that service, which has its own privacy practices.

These requests do not include your saved notes, journal photographs or a personal Amstroll account. Amstroll does not use the information for advertising or cross-app tracking. Public providers may retain request logs under their own policies; no claim of anonymous, unretained or wholly on-device processing is made for network features.

## Location and choice

Location access is requested only after you choose to use your current location. It is foreground-only, with manual Amsterdam starting areas available if you decline. You can revoke the permission in iPhone Settings. The app does not request background location or monitor your progress with GPS. Outing progress is updated manually.

The authored Amsterdam collection, its planning estimates and bundled photographs remain available without live place requests. Nearby places requires a connection; provider failures stay visible rather than silently changing the data source.
