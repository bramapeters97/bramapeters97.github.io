# Third-party notices

Amstroll's original interface, mark and authored itineraries are by Bram Peters Studio. The native app includes the real reference catalogue and licensed photographs rather than fabricated venue images.

## Fonts

Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Sans are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Complete notices are included as PlusJakartaSans-OFL.txt and DMSans-OFL.txt. The fonts are embedded for the interface and are not sold separately.

## Photographs

Each bundled photograph retains its author, original source, licence and adaptation note in photos.json and asset-manifest.json. Photo-Credits.md contains the original reference credits. Photographs have been converted from the embedded WebP assets to PNG for native use; displayed crops vary. Area photography is labelled as context, not a photograph of every suggested venue.

Nearby place photographs retain visible source and licence links. Images without an acceptable linked licence are excluded. A failed image is reported as unavailable, not replaced with unrelated scenery. No provider ratings or reviews are invented.

- OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright — ODbL 1.0 for applicable place data.
- Wikipedia contributors: https://en.wikipedia.org/ — applicable CC BY-SA terms; factual context and geotagged place references.
- Wikimedia Commons: each original file page gives its individual licence.
- Wikidata: https://www.wikidata.org/ — CC0 factual place discovery, descriptions and linked image identities.
- MET Norway: https://www.met.no/en/free-meteorological-data — attribution and CC BY 4.0 requirements apply to forecast data.
- Amsterdam municipal data: https://maps.amsterdam.nl/open_geodata/?k=649 — tram/metro inventory, not a live departure feed. Consult source terms.
- Apple Maps: nearby listings and calculated walking routes are supplied by Apple and remain distinct from authored sample allowances. Native Maps attribution remains visible on route maps.
- GVB and NS: external official journey-planner navigation, not an authenticated live timetable integration.

No third-party binary SDK is included. Core functionality is implemented with Apple frameworks. Public service terms and content licences must be reviewed again before a commercial release; third-party availability is not guaranteed.
