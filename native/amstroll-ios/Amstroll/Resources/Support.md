# Amstroll support

Amstroll helps you explore Amsterdam with a time window, a starting area and a few preferences. Estimates help compare ideas; they do not guarantee opening hours, prices, availability or transport operation.

- **Open places** means open-data discovery, not "open right now". A missing route, price or access check remains visible.
- **Amsterdam collection** is the bundled offline catalogue. Choose it explicitly from Sources to try authored plans without a connection.
- **More of these** replaces five suggestions with the next batch. The full pool reshuffles after you have browsed it.
- Location permission is optional. Pick an Amsterdam starting area if location is unavailable or denied.
- Walking effort is separate from total outing duration. Your chosen time and budget never increase silently.
- Progress is manual. Completing or skipping a stop does not mean the app has detected your arrival.
- Saved ideas and journal memories are local to this device. Keep an original copy of any photograph you want to retain independently.
- Reset removes Amstroll's local data. It does not delete the original photo in your library.
