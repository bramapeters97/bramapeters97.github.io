The Honeymoon
Webshop

When my sister and my brother-in-law got married, I built them a wedding gift website for their honeymoon in Thailand.

Give a stay or chip in

My sister and my brother-in-law wanted guests to have the option of contributing to the trip. I turned hotel stays and activities from their itinerary into individual gifts, so guests could see exactly what they were helping give. They could choose a gift outright or chip in towards an activity with an amount they felt comfortable giving.

A framed map
of Thailand

I designed a framed pin-up poster with a map of Thailand. I used pins to mark where each gifted stay or activity would take place, then put the guest’s message next to its pin.

My sister and my brother-in-law could follow their trip on the map and read the messages where they belonged. The guests’ words became part of the wedding gift they could keep.
