# Amstroll photography credits

The photographs show real Amsterdam locations and are labelled as area context. They do not imply that a photograph shows every named venue. Original sources and licenses were verified through Wikimedia Commons metadata; images are redistributed as resized, compressed WebP derivatives. Display cropping can vary. Share-alike photo derivatives retain their original license. These records do not include Google provider content.
