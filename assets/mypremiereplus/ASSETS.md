# MyPremiere+ public demo assets

All catalogue records, dates and the single Everyone viewing identity are fictional. No family photographs, personal recordings or private source filenames are included.

## Artwork

The five 1280-pixel JPEG illustrations in `artwork/` and the clapperboard `icon.svg` are allowlisted copies of the existing MyPremierePlus project's public mockup artwork. The illustrations were originally generated for that project: generic lake, coast, mountain, empty reception and empty cinema scenes. Each was visually inspected before copying. No additional stock imagery or optional font files were copied. Demos use the browser's system font stack.

## Sample video

`media/sunday-cinema.mp4` is a locally generated eight-second, silent H.264 illustrated sample. It slowly pans/zooms the generated lake artwork with a sample label. It was rendered on a 960 × 540 browser canvas at 24 fps and recorded using MediaRecorder at a requested 700 kbit/s. It contains no recorded family footage, soundtrack, third-party stock clip or remote media URL. All six fictional catalogue titles deliberately play this same file. The player explains this limitation.

## Screenshots

`screenshots/ios.png`, `ipad.png` and `tvos.png` are unretouched captures of the finished public demos in desktop Edge with fresh viewing state. They show only the app surface, generated artwork and fictional catalogue. They are browser screenshots, not evidence of native applications running on Apple hardware.

The artwork and video are served locally with the demos. No external media, font or runtime request is needed by the demos or launcher. The article retains the existing portfolio CSS and its existing external font imports.
