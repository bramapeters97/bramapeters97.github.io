# Bundled artwork

The five PNG files in `public/artwork/` were created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. They contain generic scenery and an empty interior, with no private family photographs or identifying people. They illustrate fictional demo records; they do not represent footage in the shared sample video. The optional cover studio continues to label its sample previews as demo artwork and does not claim to call a live image provider.

The code-native SVGs remain available as fallbacks. Canonical demo records reference the PNGs. Fonts are self-hosted under `public/fonts/`, with licenses and a source/hash manifest in that directory.
