# Legal and privacy completion record

Draft reviewed: 1 November 2026. This record describes the current local demonstration and outstanding deployment decisions. It is not a legal opinion, certification, a completed privacy assessment or permission to launch a commercial service.

## User facing locations

- `/about`: cinematic introduction, working illustrative footage price calculator and all policy links.
- `/legal/privacy`: data categories, access, providers, retention, rights and children.
- `/legal/terms`: demonstration scope, accounts, rights, sharing and consumer contracting gaps.
- `/legal/cookies`: first party cookie and local storage inventory.
- `/legal/legal-notice`: explicit missing operator fields.
- `/legal/content`: ownership, guest contributions, sensitive media and optional AI.
- `/legal/accessibility`: implementation aims and limits without a conformance claim.
- `/legal/billing`: example footage block calculation and no real payments.
- Administration: **Legal & policies** provides all pages, with a visible draft notice.
- The public footer identifies Pexels and Mixkit and links to the legal notice's footage credits. The authenticated admin Legal page loads per-clip source and licence credits from `/api/demo/media`.
- “MyPremierePlus is part of Bram Peters Studio” is a user-authorised public brand attribution linking to `https://bram-peters.com`. It does not establish a registered legal entity, trader, address, tax status or legal contact. The operator placeholders remain unresolved.
- Login, account and other public entry links are integrated by the application shell. Verify those links again whenever entry routes change.

## Operator fields required before launch

1. Legal operator/trader name; trading name; registered/service address; country; registration details; VAT details where applicable.
2. Monitored general, customer support, privacy, security and content complaints contacts. Establish response ownership and identity verification without excessive identity collection.
3. Controller/processor analysis for account data, hosted archive media, household use and commercial hosting. Identify any representative or DPO where required; do not invent a requirement or role.
4. Purposes and legal bases for each operation, including account security, hosting, viewing history, contributions, billing and optional processing. Assess special category content, depicted nonmembers and children's data. Consent from one uploader does not resolve all third party rights.
5. Actual processors, subprocessors, hosting locations and contractual terms. Identify external stock media delivery separately from private storage. Check Pexels/Mixkit delivery and any configured Cloudflare service, execute applicable processing terms, assess international transfers and required safeguards, and record actual retention/security arrangements. Do not claim EU only residency from a generic DPA.
6. A written, implemented retention schedule for active accounts, expired sessions/codes/tokens, content, rejected contributions, deleted rows, unreferenced assets, history snapshots, exports, logs, provider copies and backups. Add and verify purge jobs. Publish the real deletion process and account closure behaviour.
7. A rights process for access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and consent withdrawal where applicable. Archive metadata export alone is not that process. Select the relevant supervisory authority and explain the complaint route accurately.
8. Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, access review, backup/recovery, incident response and breach notification procedures. Assess whether a DPIA is required for the actual scale and sensitive processing. No blanket compliance claim should be added.
9. Child/minor and intimate-content safeguards appropriate to the actual users, including audience restriction, guardian/depicted-person permissions, reporting and urgent access restriction. Assess age/consent obligations for the real service.
10. Content licence limited to delivery of chosen features, copyright/privacy reporting, illegal-content handling, explanations/appeals and applicable hosting/DSA obligations. Have a qualified reviewer determine which duties apply rather than asserting an exemption.
11. Before a paid offer: real price/tax treatment, duration and renewal, billing timing, payment processor, cancellation, refunds, complaints, statutory remedies and accurate withdrawal information. Distinguish ongoing digital services from digital content, with any required early-performance request or express acknowledgement. Do not precheck or assume a waiver from signup/playback.
12. Actual storage, original-file, encoding, streaming, upload and fair-use limits. Footage hours in an example calculator must not imply unlimited file size or originals preservation.
13. Before external AI/transcription: provider and data description, lawful grounds and rights, explicit appropriate user choice, minors/sensitive-content review, processing/retention/transfer/training terms and a usable way to decline. Keep private media local unless a reviewed feature deliberately sends it.
14. Accessibility obligations and statement applicable to the real operator/service. Conduct keyboard, screen reader, zoom, contrast/customisation, captions, reduced motion and TV/device testing; record findings and a monitored feedback process.
15. Verify only necessary storage remains. If optional analytics/advertising/embeds are added, block nonessential storage until any legally required consent and provide equally usable controls. Do not add a decorative consent banner without a working mechanism.
16. Local font/asset/software licence attribution, security review and legal review for applicable countries. Complete these visible drafts with accurate facts; record a version/date and user notification approach for material changes.

## Primary sources consulted

These sources informed the draft's scope and outstanding questions. They do not verify the service's compliance or substitute for a legal review. Legal applicability depends on the final operator, processing and commercial offer.

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, official EUR-Lex text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/): principles and lawful grounds, Article 13 notice information, data subject rights, processor arrangements, security and international transfers. Current templates intentionally leave unknown roles, contacts, legal bases and safeguards unresolved.
- [European Commission: information for individuals](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en): clear notice content and conditional rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaints.
- [European Commission: legal grounds for processing](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/legal-grounds-processing-data_en): different bases require purpose and context assessment; do not treat general signup as consent for every operation.
- [Your Europe: e-commerce and distance selling](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm): precontract information and withdrawal requirements for consumer distance contracts; exceptions have conditions and must be assessed for the actual service.
- [Your Europe: returns and withdrawal](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm): consumer-facing explanation of the general withdrawal period and exceptions.
- [Your Europe: online privacy and cookies](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/growing/digitalising/online-privacy/index_en.htm): necessary authentication/user-request storage and optional cookies require different treatment. Actual deployed storage must be checked.
- [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/): end user network/technical processing and customer responsibilities. The draft mentions only the technical media-request implications relevant to this build; it does not borrow Cloudflare's claims as its own.
- [Cloudflare customer data processing addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/): customer content, processing instructions and transfer arrangements. Verify the agreement and safeguards adopted by the actual operator.
- [Pexels privacy policy](https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/) and [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): provider processing information and stock-use terms, including restrictions on endorsement and standalone redistribution. A direct media request is distinguished from browsing Pexels' website; the draft does not claim that every website tracker is used by a direct CDN video request.
- [Envato privacy policy applying to Mixkit](https://www.envato.com/privacy/), [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/) and [Mixkit terms](https://mixkit.co/terms/): provider processing, selected free-licensed project use and restrictions on standalone resale or stock inventories. Each chosen Mixkit page explicitly identified the free licence. Candidate URLs, response checks and beginning/mid/end playback samples are in `docs/demo-media-research-b.json`.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): reference for accessibility assessment; this project does not claim audited conformance.

Sources checked 1 November 2026. The draft uses original plain-language text, with links to primary guidance rather than copied legal boilerplate.
