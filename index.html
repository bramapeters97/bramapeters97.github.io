Lost In Translation™

What began as a fun way to practice Swedish while living in Stockholm turned into a design project powered by AI. I built a custom algorithm and a cast of quirky AI travel-assistants, each with their own sass and personality, to generate metro maps with absurdly literal, customizable and perfectly imperfect translations. Soon, anyone can create a personalized, hilariously inaccurate transit map, crafted by my highly incapable bots. Global Transit, Local Nonsense. Just mind the (language) gap while we're at it.
